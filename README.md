One deadpan weather forecast for a city that does not exist, read like a real evening broadcast: in Tumbleford, today's high arrives on a schedule, stays eleven minutes, and leaves — followed by a 40% chance of Tuesday.

> Good evening from Tumbleford, where today's high of 62 arrives at 2:15, stays eleven minutes, then leaves; a low of 61 follows, with a 40% chance of Tuesday.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the forecast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 160 characters of UTF-8 text, exactly one line, naming the city, giving a temperature and a chance, in a deadpan broadcast tone.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
